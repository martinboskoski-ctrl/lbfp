## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:**
> {{PRODUCT}} is the all-in-one operations platform that replaces the email threads,
> spreadsheets, and paper forms a {{COMPANY_TYPE}} runs on — one secure workspace for
> every department, from the shop floor to the boardroom.

**Elevator pitch:**
> Manufacturing companies don't fail for lack of effort — they leak time in the gaps
> between departments. Sales emails procurement, HR chases paper approvals, contracts
> expire unnoticed, and leadership has no single view. {{PRODUCT}} closes those gaps.
> It gives every department its own workspace — tasks, projects, documents, people —
> while role-based access keeps everyone focused on exactly what's theirs. Managers see
> their team. Leadership sees everything. And it all updates in real time.

**The problem (the "before"):**
- Work scattered across email, Excel, WhatsApp, and filing cabinets
- No visibility across departments; leadership flies blind
- Approvals and requests lost in inboxes
- Contracts and compliance deadlines missed
- Documents drafted by hand, inconsistently
- New hires onboarded with paper and tribal knowledge

**The solution (the "after"):** one platform, role-aware, real-time, bilingual, cloud.

**Target personas (who the camera should "speak to"):**
1. **Owner / Top Management** — wants total visibility and control.
2. **Department Manager** (Sales, HR, Production, QA…) — wants their team aligned.
3. **Employee** — wants one place for their tasks, requests, and documents.

## 4. Scene-by-scene script

> Format per scene — **VO:** narration · **CAPTION:** on-screen text · **VISUAL:** what we see.

### Scene 1 — Cold open / hook (0:00–0:10)
- **VO:** "Running a manufacturing business means a hundred moving parts… and most of them live in your inbox."
- **CAPTION:** *Email. Spreadsheets. Paper. Sound familiar?*
- **VISUAL:** Fast montage — overflowing inbox, a tangle of spreadsheets, a sticky-note-covered monitor, a paper approval form passed hand to hand. Slight desaturation, mild tension.

### Scene 2 — The cost (0:10–0:20)
- **VO:** "Work slips through the cracks between departments. Approvals stall. Contracts expire. And nobody has the full picture."
- **CAPTION:** *The gaps between teams are where time goes to die.*
- **VISUAL:** Split-screen of disconnected departments; a red "EXPIRED" stamp lands on a contract; a clock spins.

### Scene 3 — Product reveal (0:20–0:30)
- **VO:** "Meet {{PRODUCT}} — one secure workspace for your entire company. {{TAGLINE}}."
- **CAPTION:** *{{PRODUCT}} — {{TAGLINE}}*
- **VISUAL:** UI snaps into focus, color returns. Clean dashboard with the left sidebar of departments. {{LOGO}} animates in.

### Scene 4 — Role-based, department-scoped (0:30–0:42)
- **VO:** "Every department gets its own workspace — and everyone sees exactly what's theirs. Employees focus on their work, managers see their team, and leadership sees everything."
- **CAPTION:** *Three access levels. Zero noise.*
- **VISUAL:** Sidebar with 13 department icons; toggle between an Employee view (narrow), a Manager view (department), and a Top-Management view (all departments light up). A subtle "Manager" / "Top Management" badge.

### Scene 5 — Tasks: drag-and-drop Kanban (0:42–0:54)
- **VO:** "Plan the day on a simple board. Drag a task to move it forward — assignees, deadlines, and history all stay in sync."
- **CAPTION:** *See the work. Move the work. Done.*
- **VISUAL:** Kanban columns; a card drags from "In progress" to "Done" with a satisfying snap; an assignee avatar and due-date chip update; a small "edited" history badge.

### Scene 6 — Projects with stage gates (0:54–1:06)
- **VO:** "Bigger initiatives become projects — with subtasks, the right departments looped in, and approval gates so nothing ships before it's signed off."
- **CAPTION:** *Cross-team projects, governed start to finish.*
- **VISUAL:** A project card expands into subtasks; "Gate 1 → Gate 4" progress with green check approvals; file attachments thumbnail in.

### Scene 7 — Personal home cockpit (1:06–1:16)
- **VO:** "Each morning, everyone opens one cockpit — their tasks, their deadlines, company announcements, and a shared whiteboard."
- **CAPTION:** *Your whole day, the moment you log in.*
- **VISUAL:** Home dashboard: "My tasks", "My requests", a deadline list, a pinned urgent announcement, a collaborative whiteboard with sticky notes.

### Scene 8 — Requests & approvals (1:16–1:28)
- **VO:** "Need time off, overtime, equipment, or travel? Request it in seconds and watch it route automatically — manager, HR, finance — with leave balances kept up to date."
- **CAPTION:** *Paperless approvals with a built-in trail.*
- **VISUAL:** A "Day Off" request form submits; a stepper animates Manager → HR → Approved; a leave-balance counter ticks down.

### Scene 9 — HR employee dossiers (1:28–1:38)
- **VO:** "Every employee has a complete digital file — documents, assets, salary history, leave, and records — all in one place."
- **CAPTION:** *Your people, fully on file.*
- **VISUAL:** An employee profile with tabs for Documents, Assets, Salary history, Leave; clean record cards.

### Scene 10 — Sales CRM (1:38–1:48)
- **VO:** "Sales gets a built-in CRM — track leads, convert them to clients, and follow orders, all without leaving the platform."
- **CAPTION:** *From first lead to repeat order.*
- **VISUAL:** Leads pipeline → client profile with an orders list and a small sales-overview chart.

### Scene 11 — Contract lifecycle + Excel export (1:48–2:02)
- **VO:** "Never miss a renewal again. Every contract is tracked by sector, with automatic reminders before it expires — and one click exports the whole register to a clean, color-coded Excel workbook."
- **CAPTION:** *Contracts that remind you — before it's too late.*
- **VISUAL:** Contract register table; an amber "expires in 14 days" reminder notification slides in; the **Export to Excel** button clicks; a polished multi-sheet, color-coded spreadsheet fans out (one tab per sector, colored status cells).

### Scene 12 — Document automation (2:02–2:10)
- **VO:** "Generate NDAs, agreements, and annexes in seconds — consistent, professional, and ready to send."
- **CAPTION:** *Paperwork, drafted for you.*
- **VISUAL:** Pick a template → fill a short form → a finished .docx slides out and downloads.

### Scene 13 — Procedures, compliance & training (2:10–2:20)
- **VO:** "Keep the company aligned: a living procedures library, compliance health-checks that score your whole team, and interactive trainings with graded tests."
- **CAPTION:** *Knowledge, compliance, and training — measured.*
- **VISUAL:** Procedures list; an LHC compliance campaign with a score ring; a training module with a video and a passed quiz / certificate.

### Scene 14 — Cross-department inquiries (2:20–2:28)
- **VO:** "When a customer asks the hard questions, the right departments answer together — sales, quality, R&D, and procurement — in one structured thread."
- **CAPTION:** *Pre-order questions, answered as a team.*
- **VISUAL:** An inquiry card routes questions to department lanes; each returns a green-checked answer.

### Scene 15 — Production suite (2:28–2:36)
- **VO:** "And the shop floor runs on it too — maintenance tickets, shift schedules, and production reports with real numbers."
- **CAPTION:** *From the office to the factory floor.*
- **VISUAL:** Maintenance ticket; a weekly shift calendar; an animated production-output bar chart. Light real factory B-roll.

### Scene 16 — Cross-cutting strengths (2:36–2:44)
- **VO:** "It's real-time, secure, cloud-based — and fully bilingual in English and Macedonian."
- **CAPTION:** *Real-time · Secure cloud · Bilingual EN/MK*
- **VISUAL:** A live notification pops; a lock/secure-upload icon; an EN⇄MK language toggle flips the whole UI instantly.

### Scene 17 — Close / CTA (2:44–2:55)
- **VO:** "{{PRODUCT}}. One platform for your whole company. {{TAGLINE}}."
- **CAPTION:** *{{PRODUCT}} — {{TAGLINE}}*  ·  *{{CTA_URL}}*
- **VISUAL:** {{LOGO}} center frame on white, tagline beneath, CTA button. Music resolves.

## 9. Negative prompts / do-nots

- No jargon-stuffed captions; lead with the benefit, not the feature name.
- No fake/cluttered enterprise dashboards with unreadable fake numbers.
- No stock "hacker in a hoodie" or generic spinning-globe clichés.
- Don't overcrowd scenes — one clear idea each.
- Keep cursor movements smooth and human, not robotic.
- Avoid dark, moody color grading except briefly in the opening "problem" beat.
- Don't misstate scope: it's an internal operations platform, not a public marketplace.
